Phone Remote

Your phone is the remote for your PC.

Play, pause, skip, seek and change volume on your Windows PC from your Android phone, over your own Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. Send files of any size between any devices, privately. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Download for Windows Download Android app (APK)

Free and open source. Windows 10/11 · Android 9+

Get connected in 60 seconds

  1. Install Phone Remote on your PC and run it. It sits in the tray and shows a QR code.
  2. Scan the QR code with your phone camera or the Android app.
  3. Approve the phone on your PC. Done — it stays paired until you remove it.

No Wi-Fi? Use Bluetooth mode: the phone acts as a Bluetooth keyboard and media-key device, with no PC software needed.

Send files, too

Open one page on both devices, share the link or QR code, and pick files. They go straight from device to device, encrypted, with no size limit and nothing to install for the person receiving.

Open Send files

Built on FileSync (MIT). A small server only introduces the devices and relays encrypted data when a direct connection isn't possible; it never stores files.

Private by design

Stays on your network

Remote-control commands go straight from phone to PC. There is no server in the middle.

You approve every phone

Pairing needs your OK on the PC, and any phone can be revoked at any time.

No tracking

No accounts, analytics or ads. Files are never stored. Read the privacy policy.

Good to know

  • Until the Windows installer carries a paid code-signing certificate, Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Choose More info → Run anyway.
  • Wi-Fi traffic is plain HTTP on your home network, limited to private addresses. Don't use it on networks you don't trust.
  • Bluetooth mode can't show what's playing, and seeks with the arrow keys.
